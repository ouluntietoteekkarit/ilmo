Hellurei and hellät tunteet. Alongside the year’s most popular event, TST’s big house party, we are now introducing TST Sitsit! The sitsit will take place at Teekkaritalo on December 2 at 6 PM. The evening has a Smurf theme and is organized with students from SIK, OLTO, OTiT, and Blanko.

The sitsit will end at around 10 PM, after which the sauna will be heated, so BYOB and sauna gear. The afterparty will continue at Teekkaritalo until 2 AM and is open to all TST (ITEE) students.

Registration closes on November 22 at 2 PM. Tickets cost €21.

  • What to wear: overalls + Smurf-themed outfit
