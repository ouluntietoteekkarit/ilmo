Hellurei ja hellät tunteet. Vuoden suosituimpien bileiden, TST:n suurten talobileiden, rinnalle pamahtaa nyt TST-sitsit! TST-sitsejä vietetään 2.12. 18:00 alkaen Teekkaritalolla Smurffi-teemalla yhdessä SIK:in, OLTO:n, OTiT:in sekä Blankon opiskelijoiden kesken.

Hellurei and hellät tunteet. Alongside the year’s most popular party, the TST house party, we are now bringing you TST Sitsit! The TST sitsit will be held on December 2nd at 6 PM at Teekkaritalo with a Smurf-themed evening together with SIK, OLTO, OTiT, and Blanko.

The sitsit will end at approximately 10 PM, after which the sauna will be heated and the afterparty will continue at Teekkaritalo until 2 AM so BYOB and sauna gear are recommended. The afterparty is open to all TST students!

Ticket sales begin on November 16th at 12:00 noon via the registration form. Registration closes on November 22nd at 2 PM. The ticket costs 21 €.
